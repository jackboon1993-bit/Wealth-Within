Wealth Within

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This document accurately describes what Wealth Within actually does — read the note at the bottom before treating it as final, but everything above it reflects the real, current behaviour of the app, not generic boilerplate.

Who this is

Wealth Within is provided by Wealth Within, referred to below as "we" or "us". If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact jackboon1993@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • The financial figures you type in yourself — income, spending, mortgage and loan details, savings, pension information, and anything else you enter into the app. We never pull this from a bank or any external source; every number in your account came from you.
  • Pension documents you choose to upload — if you use the pension document reader, the PDF or photo you upload is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for that one request, so it can read and summarise it for you. We don't store the document itself anywhere; only the figures you choose to save afterwards, and only if you tap "use these numbers."
  • Feedback you choose to send us through the in-app feedback button, along with which account sent it.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, or any information beyond what's listed above unless you choose to include it in a field.

What we don't collect

  • We don't use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to a third party for marketing or profiling purposes.
  • We don't connect to your bank, and we have no technical ability to see your real account balances or transactions unless you type them in.

How we store it

Your data is held in a database provided by Supabase, hosted in the EU (Ireland). Each person's data is isolated at the database level using row-level security — the database itself enforces that your account can only ever read or write your own data. Your password is never stored in plain text; Supabase handles this using industry-standard hashing. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

We may also use an error-monitoring tool (Sentry) to catch and fix bugs. If enabled, it can capture technical details about a crash — it is not configured to identify you personally, and is not currently receiving your financial data.

Why we process it

To provide the service you signed up for: calculating your financial score, projections, and forecasts, reading pension documents you choose to upload, and saving your data so it's there next time you log in. We rely on this being necessary to provide the service you've asked for, rather than on separate marketing consent, since we don't do any marketing with it.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or provide it to any third party for their own purposes. The following process data on our behalf, only to provide the service, and don't use it for anything of their own:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Anthropic — reads a pension document you upload, only at the moment you upload it, to generate the summary shown to you.
  • Vercel — hosts the app and its server functions.
  • Sentry, if enabled — error monitoring, as above.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself, at any time, from the app's settings menu ("Delete my account") — this removes your login and your data immediately, not just your saved figures. You can also email jackboon1993@gmail.com if you'd rather we do it for you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to, and receive a portable copy of your data. To exercise any of these, email jackboon1993@gmail.com — or use the in-app deletion option for erasure specifically. If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Wealth Within is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, let account holders know.

A note on where this stands, honestly: this document accurately describes what the app currently does — no ad tech, no bank connection, no data sharing beyond the named processors above, real encryption, real isolation between accounts, and genuine self-service deletion. What it doesn't yet include is a lawyer's review for legal correctness under UK GDPR and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, or a confirmed ICO registration. Treat this as a strong, honest starting draft — not a substitute for that review before wider release.
